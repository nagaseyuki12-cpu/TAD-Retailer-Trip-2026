Visit Japan Web

Simple support page for entry registration before arrival in Japan.

Registration Guide

  1. Create or open your Visit Japan Web account.
  2. Register traveler information.
  3. Register trip details and accommodation.
  4. Complete immigration and customs information.
  5. Save the QR code on your phone.

FAQ

Do I need to complete this before departure?

Recommended. Please complete it before your flight to reduce stress at arrival.

What if I make a mistake?

Contact the organizer using the Help button. The organizer can guide you on what to check.

Important Reminder

Please keep your passport, flight information and hotel address ready when completing the registration.
