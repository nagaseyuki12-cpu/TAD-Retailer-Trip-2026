Preparation

Packing, money, internet, smoking rules and power adapter information.

Packing Checklist

  • Passport
  • Travel insurance information
  • Comfortable business casual clothes
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Personal medicine
  • Phone charger and power adapter

Cash and Card

Japan is card-friendly in many places, but guests should also carry some cash for small shops, taxis or personal expenses.

Smoking Regulations

Smoking is allowed only in designated smoking areas. Please do not smoke while walking on the street unless signs clearly permit it.

Power Adapter

Japan uses Type A / Type B plugs. Please prepare an adapter if your device uses a different plug type.
